Designed for real-world biometric workflows

From investigating rejected files to developing biometric integrations, NIST Editor helps you inspect record content, understand structural issues and troubleshoot interoperability using supported standards and profiles.

  • ANSI/NIST-ITL 1-2025
  • ANSI/NIST-ITL 1-2011
  • FBI EBTS
  • INTERPOL
  • Optional imported implementation profiles
  • Traditional and XML record handling

Integrated NFIQ2 fingerprint quality analysis

Assess suitable fingerprint images locally, inspect minutiae and other diagnostics, and visualize analysis results over the decoded fingerprint used for processing.

  • Fingerprint quality score and assessment
  • Ridge endings and bifurcations
  • Minutiae statistics
  • Overlay visualization
  • CSV and PNG export support

Your biometric data stays local

Supported editing, decoding, validation, conversion and quality analysis take place on your computer. No cloud upload or online processing is required for these functions.
